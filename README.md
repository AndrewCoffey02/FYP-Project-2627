# Crohn's Disease health tracking application

Crohn’s disease is a chronic inflammatory bowel disease that can affect patients' digestive health, nutritional status and quality of life. Patients may experience varying symptoms and food tolerances, making it useful to record dietary intake and symptoms over time. However, the relationships between diet and symptoms can be complex and differ between individuals.

To what extent can dietary and lifestyle data be used to identify patterns in self-reported symptom severity among people with Crohn’s disease?

## Application
- Research existing digital health applications relevant to Crohn’s disease and dietary monitoring.
- Design and implement a user-friendly dietary and symptom-tracking application.

## Model Integration
- Develop and compare suitable machine-learning models for identifying associations between dietary data and reported symptom patterns.
- Evaluate the models using appropriate validation methods and performance metrics.
- Present findings through interactive charts and personalised summaries.

## Proposed Technologies
- Python (Numpy + Matplotlib)
- Scikit-learn
- SQLite
- Streamlit (Demo Application)
